What is the Festival of Faiths?

The Ismaili Council and Ismaili Center invite you to join us for the inaugural Festival of Faiths Houston on Sunday, October 4, 2026.

The Festival of Faiths brings together people from across Houston's diverse faith communities to build understanding, forge meaningful connections, and celebrate the values we share. Through inspiring music, captivating dance, visual arts, storytelling, thought-provoking panels, and engaging presentations, the festival creates space for people of different faiths and backgrounds to learn from one another, celebrate our diversity, and strengthen the bonds that connect us as a community.
