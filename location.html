Event Location & Parking

Parking

Please use the West Dallas entrance (2290 West Dallas Street) to access the Ismaili Center. The security guard will direct you to the underground parking garage. Park on Lower Level 1 or 2 and take the white elevators to Level 1 to reach the check-in desk.

Tours will be available.
